# EatMate 🥗

**Your simple food diary — no calorie counting.**

🌍 [Português](README.md) · **English** · [Español](README.es.md) · [Deutsch](README.de.md) · [Français](README.fr.md) · [Italiano](README.it.md)

Write down what you ate the way you'd say it — _"a cup of coffee and two slices of toast"_ — and EatMate figures out the foods. After 7 days a **thermometer** shows the quality of your diet as a grade from **A to E** (Nutri-Score style), plus a **full report** to show your dietitian or paste into an AI (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude…) for tips.

## 👉 Open EatMate

### **[malonefreak.github.io/EatMate](https://malonefreak.github.io/EatMate/)**

Just open the link on your phone or computer. **Nothing to download from an app store, no account, no payment.** After the first visit it even works offline.

- **Computer (Chrome or Edge):** click the install icon in the address bar.

## ✍️ How it works

1. **Tell it your name.** That's it — no email, no password.
2. **Tap "Log a meal"** and type what you ate in your own words. EatMate splits the foods, suggests as you type and shows each food's grade. Log right away or later, on any past day.
3. **Made a mistake?** In **History** you can edit or delete any meal.
4. **After 7 days** of logging, the **thermometer** appears on the home screen with your score (0–100 and A–E).
5. **Tap the thermometer** for the full report: what went well, what to watch, fruits and vegetables, ultra-processed foods, meal regularity and your complete food diary. **Copy it for an AI**, **save it as a PDF** or **share** it.
6. **Every week** a report is saved in **Reports**. Fixed a day later? Generate the report again with one tap.

## 🌍 Languages

EatMate speaks **Portuguese, English, Spanish, German, French and Italian**. It opens in your phone's language, and you can switch anytime in **Profile → Language** — including the food names it understands.

## 🔒 Your data stays yours

No sign-up, no server, no ads, no trackers. Everything stays **on your device** and only leaves it when **you** copy, share or save a report. Switching phones? Use **Profile → Export backup**, then **Restore a backup** on the new phone.

> ⚠️ Grades are estimates to help you notice patterns. EatMate does not replace advice from a dietitian.

New versions are listed under [Releases](https://github.com/MaloneFreak/EatMate/releases); the app tells you when an update is available. Found a problem? [Open an issue](https://github.com/MaloneFreak/EatMate/issues).
